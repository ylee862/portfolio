About

Yedam Lee

I design outward from friction: the moment someone hesitates, gets stuck, or quietly gives up.

My habit is opening the apps I use every day and noticing where I hesitate. Then I ask whether it's a design problem or a product one: a confusing screen, or a feature that shouldn't exist yet.

Because I can code, I take my own designs all the way to a working product. That keeps me honest about trade-offs, and I'm as willing to cut scope as I am to add polish.

Every project, I ask the same three questions: what problem is this solving, is it the right problem right now, and what am I trading off by shipping it this way?
