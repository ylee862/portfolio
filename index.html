About

Research-driven.
Built to ship.

I studied Computer Science, but got hooked on the layer above code: why people struggle with products, and which problems are actually worth solving. Today I move between research, product decisions, interaction design, and the code that ships it.

My habit is opening apps I use daily, noticing where I hesitate or get stuck, and asking whether that's a design problem or a product one: a confusing screen, or a feature that shouldn't exist yet. I design outward from that friction, and I'm just as willing to cut scope as I am to add polish.

Every redesign, I ask: what problem is this solving, is it the right problem right now, and what am I trading off by shipping it this way?
